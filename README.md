# 📊 E-Commerce Sales & CustomeAnalytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce business dataset using SQL and Power BI to identify sales trends, profitability issues, customer behavior, and business growth opportunities.

The objective of the project is to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights through data analysis and interactive visualizations.
---

# 🎯 Business Problem
The business was experiencing increasing sales but inconsistent profitability.

This project focuses on:
- Identifying profit leakage areas
- Understanding customer purchasing behavior
- Evaluating discount impact on profitability
- Analyzing repeat vs one-time customers
- Providing data-driven business recommendations

# 💡 Key Business Insights
- Furniture category shows high sales but low profitability.
- Higher discounts negatively impact profit margins.
- Majority customers belong to low-value segment.
- Repeat customers contribute majority of revenue.
- West region drives highest sales but inconsistent profits.
---

# 📈 Business Recommendations
- Reduce excessive discounting in low-margin categories.
- Improve customer retention strategies.
- Focus on high-value repeat customers.
- Optimize pricing strategy for low-profit sub-categories.
